Payouts

Each team pays a $150 buy-in at the start of the season. All funds go toward league prizes and are paid to the winners at the end of the season.

  • Champion: $1350 (Playoff Winner)
  • Runner-up: $300 (Loses in Finals)
  • Regular Season Points Leader: $150 (Most PF after Week 14)
  • Weekly High Score: $70 (Paid each week)
  • Trade money: $80 (Highest points for induvidual player in the last week)
